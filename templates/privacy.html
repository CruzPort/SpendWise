Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you use the app, we store the expense records you add — amount, category, date, and any description you provide.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Show you your expense history and spending summaries
  • Keep your data associated with your account across sessions

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database and are only accessible to you when logged in. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never saved as plain text.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third parties. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to run the application, but they do not have independent access to your data beyond what is required to operate the service.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
